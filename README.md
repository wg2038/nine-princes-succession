## 🎭 Presentation Overview

This presentation is built for American professionals and global history enthusiasts. It frames China's legendary "Nine Princes Contending for the Succession" (九子夺嫡) as a 47-year political thriller combining the corporate boardroom tension of *Succession* with the dynastic stakes of *House of the Dragon*.

### Core Specifications
- **Framework**: Standalone responsive presentation powered by [Reveal.js](https://revealjs.com/).
- **Aesthetic**: Cinematic Imperial Luxury — Dark imperial lacquer (`#0a0a0e`), deep vermilion red (`#8b1e1e`), and burnished gold (`#d4af37`).
- **Typography**: Display serif (`Cinzel`, `Playfair Display`) paired with crisp sans-serif (`Inter`) and classical calligraphy (`Noto Serif SC`).
- **Assets**: Authentic high-resolution historical court portraits and imperial wills from the Beijing Palace Museum and First Historical Archives of China, bundled locally with CDN fallbacks.

## 📑 Slide Outline (12 Slides)

1. **Slide 1: Title & Hook (Prologue)**  
   *The Nine Sons of the Dragon* · 47 years of intrigue, 35 imperial sons, 9 contenders, 1 throne.
2. **Slide 2: The Stage & The Patriarch (Emperor Kangxi)**  
   Profile of China's "Sun King" (61-year reign) and the institutional clash between Han Confucian primogeniture (Crown Prince Yinreng) and Manchu meritocratic warlord tradition (八旗八议).
3. **Slide 3: The Board: 9 Contenders & 5 Factions (Interactive Dossier)**  
   Interactive faction matrix categorizing the princes:
   - *Faction 1*: Crown Prince Yinreng (The Broken Heir)
   - *Faction 2*: 1st Prince Yinzhi (The Eldest Warrior) & 3rd Prince Yinzhi (The Scholar)
   - *Faction 3*: 4th Prince Yinzhen (Future Yongzheng) & 13th Prince Yinxiang (The Loyal General)
   - *Faction 4*: 8th Prince Yinsi (The Charismatic Mastermind), 9th Prince (Financier), 10th Prince (Enforcer)
   - *Faction 5*: 14th Prince Yinti (The Grand General / Western Commander)
4. **Slide 4: Crisis I: The Fall of the Heir (1708)**  
   The breakdown at the Saihanba hunting grounds, midnight tent-peeping paranoia, the deposition, and the exposure of 1st Prince's voodoo sorcery (魇胜之术).
5. **Slide 5: The Overplay of the 8th Prince (1708–1709)**  
   The open recommendation challenge: Why 90% court consensus terrified Kangxi and provoked an autocratic counter-strike against the "court oligarchy."
6. **Slide 6: Crisis II: The Final Fall & The Decade of Shadows (1712)**  
   The aborted coup with Gendarmerie Commander Tuoba, the permanent imprisonment of Yinreng, and Kangxi's vow to never appoint an heir again (10 years of total silence).
7. **Slide 7: The Master of Stealth: Prince 4's Strategy (1712–1722)**  
   *Tao Guang Yang Hui* (韬光养晦): Playing the detached Buddhist hermit ("The Hermit of Broken Screen") while solving national treasury deficits and capturing key logistical chokepoints (Longkodo & Nian Gengyao).
8. **Slide 8: The Winter Climax: The Midnight Succession (Dec 1722)**  
   The sudden death of Kangxi at Changchun Garden, the capital lockdown, and the **Definitive Debunking of the Forgery Myth** (comparing the "14 to 4" folk legend against Qing imperial grammar and bilingual Manchu script *juwan duici* vs *duici*).
9. **Slide 9: The Reckoning & The Purge (1722–1726)**  
   Consolidation of power: Rewarding 13th Prince (Iron-Cap Prince Yi), disarming 14th Prince at the frontier, and renaming 8th & 9th Princes to *Akesina* (Dog) and *Sesihe* (Pig) before their deaths in confinement.
10. **Slide 10: Historical Legacy: The Box Behind the Plaque (1723 Onward)**  
    The invention of the **Secret Succession System** (秘密立储制度): Two identical decrees—one on the Emperor's body, one sealed behind the *Zheng Da Guang Ming* (正大光明) plaque in the Palace of Heavenly Purity.
11. **Slide 11: Strategic Lessons: Power & Governance**  
    Four enduring political principles: The Succession System Dilemma, The Coalition Trap, The Quiet Executor vs Loud Favorite, and Chokepoint Logistics.
12. **Slide 12: Epilogue & Executive Dossier**  
    Definitive matrix tracking all 9 princes, their peak ranks, strategies, and final fates.

## 🎮 Interactive Features & Controls

| Key / Control | Action | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Space** / **→** / **↓** | Next | Advances to the next slide or reveals the next fragment |
| **←** / **↑** | Previous | Navigates back to the previous slide |
| **S** | Speaker Notes | Opens the comprehensive Speaker Notes window with presenter script |
| **F** | Fullscreen | Toggles cinematic full-screen view |
| **O** / **Esc** | Overview Mode | Displays a visual grid of all 12 slides for instant jumping |
| **?** | Help Modal | Displays keyboard shortcuts and interactive guide |
| **🔔 Sound Button** | Audio FX | Toggles synthesized ceremonial bronze bell & bamboo sounds via Web Audio API (zero audio files needed) |
| **Interactive Faction Cards** | Click | Click buttons on Slide 3 to inspect faction dossiers, flaws, and allies |
| **Interactive Myth Buster** | Click | Click tabs on Slide 8 to compare the urban legend vs classical Chinese vs Manchu script |
| **Interactive Plaque Box** | Click | Click the "正大光明" plaque on Slide 10 to reveal the hidden imperial decree |

## 🚀 Quick Start / How to Run

### Method 1: Direct File Launch (No Server Required)
Simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer, or open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). All CSS, JS, and image assets are bundled locally.

### Method 2: Local Web Server
You can also launch a lightweight local server from this directory:
```bash
# Python 3
python3 -m http.server 8000

# Or Node.js
npx serve .
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your web browser.
